I am a PhD student in Economics at the University of Auckland. My research interests lie in labour economics, applied microeconometrics, and gender inequality.

My current research examines how labour-market institutions and public policies—including unionisation, working-time flexibility, and childcare support—shape wages, employment, and gender inequality.

I use longitudinal and administrative data together with applied microeconometric methods, including panel-data models, instrumental variables, event-study designs, and dynamic difference-in-differences.

My broader research interests include:

- Labour economics
- Applied econometrics
- Gender economics
- Family and education policy
- Labour-market institutions
